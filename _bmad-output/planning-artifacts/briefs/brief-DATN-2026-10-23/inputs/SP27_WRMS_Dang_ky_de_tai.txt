Vietnamese: Hệ thống tiếp nhận, phân công và xử lý phản ánh hiện trường của người dân tại phường Hòa XuânAbbreviation: WRMS
3.2. Context: (brief introduction)
Residents of Hoa Xuan Ward are usually the first to notice problems in public spaces, such as illegal dumping, sidewalk encroachment, damaged roads, blocked drains or broken street lights, but they lack a quick channel to report them to the Ward People's Committee and follow the result. Ward staff receive such reports through visits, phone calls and messages, often several about the same site, and must decide manually what to handle first. This project digitizes the process by which the Ward People's Committee receives, assigns and handles field reports from residents. Residents submit reports with a category, photos and location; the system screens invalid submissions and groups reports of the same site, and for the pilot category suggests a priority level from the photos; the ward then receives, assigns and tracks each report until it is resolved. Scope: field reports from residents to the People's Committee of Hoa Xuan Ward, with a web application for Admin and ward officers and a mobile application for residents and task assignees. In this phase, automatic priority assessment is piloted for environmental sanitation reports; reports in other categories follow the same workflow, with priority set by ward officers. Complaints and denunciations (handled under separate legal procedures), administrative procedure applications, transfer of reports to city-level agencies and community engagement features (points, rankings, rewards) are out of scope for this phase.
Objectives: 
- Provide residents of Hoa Xuan Ward with a mobile channel to submit field reports with a category, photos, description and location, and to follow each report until it is resolved.
- Automatically screen invalid reports and group reports of the same site for all categories, and suggest a priority level from photos for environmental sanitation reports, so that reports are handled in the right order.
- Digitize the workflow of receiving, assigning, handling and verifying reports between Admin, ward officers and task assignees (staff of relevant departments or volunteers).
- Provide a hotspot map, dashboard and statistics by category, status and area to support management decisions.

3.3. Research Orientation
3.3.1. Research problem
Briefly describe the scientific or practical problem that the project aims to address.
Residents' photos vary in category and quality, many reports describe the same site, and some submissions are invalid. Reviewing them manually to decide what to handle first is slow. The project studies how to automatically screen invalid reports, recognize reports of the same site submitted by different people, and estimate the priority of environmental sanitation reports from their photos.
3.3.2. Research objectives
The project is expected to achieve the following research objectives:
- Recognize common and special waste types in photos of environmental sanitation reports taken in Hoa Xuan Ward.
- Define and validate priority criteria for environmental sanitation reports based on the variety of waste and special waste types.
- Develop a method to group reports of the same site using location, category and photos, applicable to all categories.
- Develop a screening step for invalid or repeated submissions in all report categories.

3.5. Expected features
- Account Registration: Residents must register an account before submitting reports; staff accounts are created by Admin.
- Field Report Submission: Submit a report with a category, a description and one or more photos taken directly or chosen from the gallery; location comes from GPS, the photo's EXIF data, or a pin on the map. Report categories (for example environmental sanitation, urban order, roads and drainage, street lighting) are managed by Admin.
- Automatic Report Analysis: Screen invalid photos and group reports of the same site for all categories; assess the priority level (Low / Medium / High) for environmental sanitation reports. Reports in other categories receive their priority level from ward officers.
- Report Review and Intake: Admin reviews the clearest report of each group, views other reports at the same location, splits wrongly grouped reports, decides on flagged images (removed 48 hours after upload), and rejects invalid reports with a reason sent to the reporter (removed within 48 hours).
- Ward Officer Handling: Ward officers receive reports assigned by Admin, adjust the priority level, verify sites in the field, propose assignees or teams, contact residents for more information, monitor progress and inspect sites after handling.
- Task Assignment: Admin assigns tasks to individuals or to a team with a team leader, and can set a deadline; overdue tasks are reported to Admin.
- Task Acceptance and Rejection: Assignees accept a task (the report moves to In Progress) or reject it with a reason; if Admin accepts the reason the task is reassigned, otherwise the assignee continues.
- Completion Evidence and Approval: One team member uploads mandatory after-handling photos; Admin approves (report Completed) or returns the task to the team for rework.
- Status Tracking and Notifications: Reports go through four statuses (Submitted, Received, In Progress, Completed); reporters, including those of grouped reports, are notified at every status change.
- Volunteer Registration: Residents apply to become volunteers; Admin verifies and approves or rejects the application and can suspend or revoke the volunteer role.
- Hotspot Map, Dashboard and Statistics: Admin only: map of report density in the ward and statistics by category, status, priority, area, handling time, overdue and rejected tasks.
