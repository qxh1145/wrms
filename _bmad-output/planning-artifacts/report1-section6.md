## 6. Project Scope & Limitations

WRMS digitizes the handling of on-site environmental reports at Hoa Xuan Ward, from the moment a citizen submits a report to the moment a ward officer accepts the result. The scope covers five environmental categories: (1) waste and sanitation, (2) water pollution, (3) air pollution and odor, (4) noise, and (5) drainage and residential sanitation. The system has three front ends: a Zalo Mini App for citizens, a mobile app for unit officers who handle tasks in the field, and a role-based web application for ward officers, ward leaders and administrators. All AI outputs are suggestions; a ward officer makes every final decision.

### 6.1 Major Features

FE-01: Citizen Reporting. Through the Zalo Mini App, citizens submit a report in one of the five environmental categories with photos, a description and a location (device GPS or a pin on the map), give consent to personal-data processing, and track the status of their own reports in the Mini App.

FE-02: AI Analysis. For waste and drainage reports, an image AI module flags invalid photos (not a real scene, unreadable, duplicate), proposes grouping of reports about the same site, and suggests a priority level. The other three categories use keyword and report-type rules. Reports are always accepted into the queue, even when the AI module is unavailable.

FE-03: Report Intake. Ward officers review reports grouped by site, confirm or reject grouping suggestions, accept or reject reports with a reason, set the final priority, request more information, and close reports that are outside the ward's jurisdiction with a note to the citizen.

FE-04: Task Routing. A configurable rule table suggests a lead unit and supporting units for each report group (by report type, keywords, submission time and AI image labels). After the officer confirms, the system creates one parallel task per unit. Ward police are handled in off-system coordination mode: the officer contacts them directly and records the outcome.

FE-05: Task Tracking. Ward officers assign tasks to an individual or a team, set deadlines (default 10 working days, 1 working day for urgent cases, configurable), reassign or revoke tasks, and track whether the assignee has read, processed and replied. The system sends reminders before the deadline and marks overdue tasks.

FE-06: Field Execution. Through the staff mobile app, unit officers receive task notifications and deadline reminders, accept or decline tasks with a reason, and submit before/after photo evidence from the site. Ward officers compare before/after photos, approve or return the work, and the citizen sees the result in the Mini App.

FE-07: Hazard Safety. When the AI module (waste category) or an officer detects signs of hazardous or medical waste (needles, chemicals, batteries), the system raises a red alert to all ward officers and locks task assignment until an officer confirms or removes the flag, so no officer is sent to clean it up by hand.

FE-08: Monitoring. Ward officers view a heat map of reports and statistics by category, area, unit, processing time and overdue tasks. Ward leaders (Chairman, Vice Chairmen) have read-only access to all reports.

FE-09: Administration. Administrators manage staff accounts, the two-level catalog (category, report type), ward units and their mode, routing rules and default deadlines, without access to report content.

### 6.2 Limitations & Exclusions

LI-01: WRMS is used only for Hoa Xuan Ward and only for the five environmental categories listed above. Sidewalks, roads, street lights and other urban-order issues are out of scope.

LI-02: WRMS handles reception and coordination within the ward only. It does not assign tasks to, integrate with, or track the progress of agencies outside the ward. Reports beyond the ward's authority are closed as "Out of jurisdiction"; any escalation to higher levels is done by the ward outside the system.

LI-03: WRMS does not replace and does not receive reports from existing city channels such as the 1022 hotline, the Gop y Da Nang app, or Zalo Official Account chat.

LI-04: AI is advisory only. It never rejects a report, assigns a task, or confirms a hazardous-waste flag on its own. Image-based AI covers only waste and drainage reports; routing uses a rule table, not machine learning. No external AI API is called.

LI-05: WRMS does not handle complaints, denunciations or administrative procedures. It does not issue violation records, identify polluters, impose penalties, or manage hazardous-waste collection documents.

LI-06: The project delivers a demo, not a production release. The Zalo Mini App runs in Development/Testing mode and can only be opened by accounts registered as Developer/Admin of the Mini App. Push notifications and chat via Zalo Official Account are future work; citizens check status inside the Mini App.

LI-07: The system is designed for about 100 reports per month at the ward. Public display of reports, community features (points, rankings, volunteers), and citizens editing or withdrawing submitted reports are not included.
